# Getting started

## Signing in

1. Open the app URL in your browser.
2. Enter your **email address** and **password**, then click **Sign in**.
3. If multi-factor authentication (MFA) is enabled on your account, you will be taken to a second screen. Enter the 6-digit code from your authenticator app and click **Verify**.

> **First time?** You will receive an invite email from HR. Click **Accept invitation** in the email and follow the prompts to set your password.

---

## Accepting an invite

1. Open the invite email and click **Accept invitation**.
2. On the page that opens, enter a password of your choice (minimum 12 characters).
3. Click **Set password**. You will be signed in automatically.

---

## Forgot your password

1. On the sign-in page click **Forgot password**.
2. Enter your email address and click **Send reset link**.
3. Open the email and click the reset link (valid for 24 hours).
4. Enter a new password and click **Reset password**.

---

## Multi-factor authentication (MFA)

MFA adds a second verification step at sign-in. It is optional unless your organisation requires it.

### Enable MFA

1. Sign in and go to **Profile** in the sidebar.
2. Scroll to the **Multi-factor authentication** card and click **Enable MFA**.
3. Scan the QR code with an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Authy, 1Password, etc.), or enter the secret key manually.
4. Enter the 6-digit code shown in the app and click **Confirm**.
5. **Save the recovery codes** that appear — they will not be shown again. Each code can be used once if you lose access to your authenticator.

### Sign in with MFA

After entering your password you will be redirected to the MFA challenge page. Enter the current 6-digit code and click **Verify**.

### Disable MFA

1. Go to **Profile**.
2. Click **Disable MFA**.
3. Enter your current password and a valid 6-digit code, then click **Confirm disable**.

---

## Signing out

Click your name or avatar in the top bar and select **Sign out**. Your session is immediately revoked.

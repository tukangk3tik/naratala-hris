# Profile & settings

**Who can access:** all roles  
**Sidebar:** Profile

---

## Viewing your profile

Click **Profile** at the bottom of the sidebar. The profile page shows your email address and current role.

---

## Changing your language

On the Profile page, use the **Language** selector to switch between:

- English (EN)
- Indonesian (ID)

The change takes effect immediately and is saved to your account.

---

## Changing your password

1. Go to **Profile**.
2. Scroll to the **Change password** card.
3. Enter your **Current password**.
4. Enter your **New password** (minimum 12 characters).
5. Click **Update password**.

If successful, all other active sessions are automatically invalidated and you remain signed in on the current device.

---

## Multi-factor authentication (MFA)

See [Getting started — MFA](./01-getting-started.md#multi-factor-authentication-mfa) for full setup and disable instructions.

At a glance:

| State | Card shows |
|---|---|
| MFA off | "Enable MFA" button |
| Setup in progress | QR code + 6-digit code input |
| MFA on | "Disable MFA" button; recovery codes already saved |
| Disabling | Password + code form |

Keep your recovery codes somewhere safe (password manager, printed sheet). Each code is single-use and allows you to sign in without your authenticator if the device is lost.

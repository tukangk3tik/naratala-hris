# Departments

**Who can view:** everyone  
**Who can create / edit / delete:** HR, Admin

---

## Viewing departments

Click **Departments** in the sidebar. The page shows a list of all departments with their name and creation date.

---

## Creating a department

1. Click **Departments** in the sidebar.
2. Click **Add department**.
3. Enter the department name.
4. Click **Save**.

---

## Editing a department

1. Click the department row or its **Edit** button.
2. Update the name.
3. Click **Save**.

---

## Deleting a department

1. Click the department row.
2. Click **Delete**.
3. Confirm the deletion.

> A department cannot be deleted while it has employees assigned to it. Reassign or remove the employees first.
